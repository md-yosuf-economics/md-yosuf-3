Privacy and documents

No cookies and no tracking. Certificates and letters are shown for viewing only.

Visitor data

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. Its pages, styles and scripts come from the site itself. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep ordinary server logs.

If you email or message Md. Yosuf, the details you send are used only to reply.

Documents

Certificates, letters and magazine pages are shown for viewing only. They open in an on-screen viewer; saving, dragging, copying and printing are switched off, and each image carries a faint watermark. Search engines are asked not to index these images.

No web page can fully stop a determined person from copying what appears on a screen. These steps stop casual saving and reuse; they are not a guarantee.

Verified copies, and contact details of the issuing organisations, are available on request by email.

Copyright

Text and photographs on this site are © 2026 Md. Yosuf and may not be reproduced without permission. Certificates and letters remain the property of the organisations that issued them. Journal articles and conference papers belong to their publishers and authors; the links on the Publications page lead to the published versions.
