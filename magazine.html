Magazine publication

A first-person article in Bengali, published in the second issue of অনুরণন (Anuranan).

Magazine
অনুরণন (Anuranan), issue 2 (২য় সংখ্যা)
Publisher
Begumganj–Sonaimuri Upazila Students’ Welfare Association
Article
ফ্যাসিবাদের কারাগারে (“In the Prison of Fascism”)
Author
Md. Yosuf, Govt. Titumir College, Dhaka
Language
Bengali

A first-person account of his arrest and time in detention during the July 2024 student uprising in Bangladesh. It appeared in the second issue of অনুরণন, the literary magazine of the Begumganj–Sonaimuri Upazila Students’ Welfare Association. He was the association’s associate literary editor from April 2023 to March 2024 (see Leadership).
