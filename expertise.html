Expertise

Four areas where the work can be checked: quantitative research, scholarly writing, teaching, and academic and career consulting.

Quantitative research design

Designing a study, collecting the data and analysing it.

  • Survey instrument development and fieldwork
  • Analysis in SPSS and Stata: descriptive statistics, chi-square tests and regression
  • Hypothesis testing and inferential statistics
  • Data collection with KoboToolbox
  • Systematic literature review, with references managed in Zotero

Seen in Environmental awareness and Smartphone addiction, and taught at Sunaru Academy.

Scholarly writing

Turning data and reading into proposals, reports and papers.

  • Peer-reviewed journal articles and a conference paper
  • Research proposals, including a first-place proposal in a club contest
  • Technical reports on road accident causation and safety interventions
  • Literature synthesis and reference management

Seen in Publications, the ARI internship and Honours.

Teaching and mentoring

Explaining economics and research methods to undergraduates and early researchers.

  • Undergraduate microeconomics, macroeconomics, statistics, mathematics and econometrics
  • Workshops on research methodology and on SPSS and Stata
  • Mentoring students on research proposals and manuscripts

Seen in Experience: Rubel’s Economics Method (2023–2025), the GTCRC, Sunaru Academy and the EMCRS SPSS course.

Academic and career consulting

Helping students and professionals present their work and apply for study and jobs.

  • CVs, résumés, cover letters and LinkedIn profiles
  • Statements of purpose and higher-study support
  • Research proposals, thesis structure and reports
  • SPSS and Stata results explained in plain language

Seen in YS Scholaria and the Experience page.

Previous Education BSS in Economics, University of Dhaka; HSC (Alim) in Humanities. Next Skills Statistical software, research methods, tools and languages.
